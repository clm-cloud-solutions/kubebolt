# KubeBolt 2.5.1 — what an escalated conversation cost

Drop-in over 2.5.0. No schema change, no migration, no new setting. The agent
stays on **1.4.6**.

## An escalated conversation was priced at the stronger model

2.5.0 added the escalation model: a conversation moves to a stronger model the
first time Kobi leaves the selected cluster. KubeBolt's cost estimate priced
the whole turn at the model it ended on, so the rounds the configured model
answered before the move were counted at the stronger model's price. A turn of
five rounds on Claude Haiku 5.5 and two on Claude Sonnet 5.5 showed **$0.456**;
it cost **$0.218**.

What your provider bills was never affected: the error was in KubeBolt's
estimate, not in the calls it made.

Each turn now records the model it moved from, and every estimate splits the
turn by model, each part at its own price:

- **Administration → AI (Kobi) → Usage**: the summary, the chart, the session
  list and the breakdowns.
- **`kubebolt_kobi_copilot_tokens_total` and
  `kubebolt_kobi_copilot_cost_usd_total`**: each model's tokens and cost under
  its own `model` label. The Kobi Health tab and the `KobiSpendAboveLastWeek`
  alert read them.

The session detail now names both models
(`claude-haiku-5-5 → claude-sonnet-5-5`), why the conversation moved, what each
model cost over which rounds, and the cache writes. Those are most of an
escalated turn's cost: each model has its own prompt cache, and the move writes
the conversation into the stronger model's.

Turns recorded before 2.5.1 did not keep the model they moved from. They keep
their old estimate, and their detail shows why they moved without the split.

## Upgrading

```bash
helm upgrade kubebolt oci://ghcr.io/clm-cloud-solutions/kubebolt/helm/kubebolt --version 2.5.1
```

The agent needs no upgrade: 2.5.1 runs with agent 1.4.6.

Do not use `--reuse-values` across chart versions; pass your values file
(`-f`) or `--reset-then-reuse-values`.
